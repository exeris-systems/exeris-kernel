grant, and this file states what we consider reasonable and customary so you do not
have to ask.

- Use the name in documentation, articles, talks, courses, and comparisons —
  including critical ones.
- Keep the name in unmodified copies you redistribute, as Apache-2.0 section 4
  requires you to.
- Use the name in an artifact or package identifier that describes a genuine
  integration, e.g. `acme-exeris-adapter`.

Nominative use is the rule: name the thing to refer to the thing.

If you modify the software and distribute it, please give it your own name. That is
the ordinary open-source expectation and it protects your users as much as ours: they
should know whose build they are running.

## Why this is not a restriction on the code

A trademark protects the *identity* of the project, not its *functionality*. Nothing
here narrows the Apache-2.0 grant, and nothing here is a condition of that licence —
if you breach this policy the licence still stands; what you face is a trademark
claim, not a copyright one. The two are separate, deliberately.

## Holder and status

"Exeris Systems" is the trading identity under which this project is published. The
marks are held by the project's founder pending incorporation, at which point the
company will succeed to them. Registration has not been sought; a mark is enforceable
from use, and registration strengthens enforcement rather than creating the claim.
Use "Exeris™" when a symbol is appropriate — "®" is reserved for registered marks and
would be inaccurate today.
